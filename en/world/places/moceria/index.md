Moceria is a vast industrial city in Baustra and the campaign’s starting point.
It spreads densely across an arid landscape, packed with brick neighbourhoods,
factories, steelworks, warehouses, railway yards and enough chimneys to blur
the horizon with steam and smoke. Its population is estimated at roughly half
a million.

From the streets, the city seems endless. Its scale becomes clearer when the
train leaves Moceria along an elevated railway bridge: neighbourhoods,
factories and freight lines arrange themselves far below before dissolving into
the dust of the basin.
