Alessa is about twenty-five, short and slim, and attractive enough for her red
lips and two blonde ponytails to command attention before she begins to speak.
She has a lively, explosive presence: she can seem charming one moment and bark
a sharp order the next. She is used to people moving when she speaks.

Her clothes combine a pale shirt richly decorated at the collar and cuffs, a
corseted waist and a short skirt. The outfit recalls the ornamental elegance of
a maid, but its materials, finish and the way she wears it speak of money and
authority, not service. She leads the prison recruits to the station, imposes
the rules of the journey and issues them the contingent’s clothes.
