## The Sacred Current

To the Custodians, Electra is not a distant queen ruling lightning from the sky. She is the presence that flows through all things: the storm, the magnetism of minerals, the impulse of nerves and the spark that awakens a machine. Lightning is not necessarily her anger, but the most violent and visible manifestation of a current that is always there.

We believe the body is a living circuit. Bones anchor it to the earth, blood carries its energy and nerves are Electra’s tiny pathways. Illness is not a moral punishment, but an interruption, a wound or an imbalance in the current. That is why healing a body and repairing a machine are related acts: both require understanding where energy should flow and restoring its course.

Science does not contradict faith. To measure, experiment and discover a natural law is to learn to read Electra’s writing. A correct formula is a prayer that can be demonstrated; a well-executed operation is a liturgy performed with the hands. Knowledge becomes profane only when it is hidden, falsified or used to subjugate others.

Before praying, operating or beginning delicate work, we wash our hands and mouths with clean water. We strike a copper plate three times, remembering the three shapes of the mitsudomoe, then keep silent for a few moments to listen to our own pulse. We offer water, copper wire, repaired tools or the first charge of a new machine. Lightning-struck trees, conductors that have survived a storm and certain old workshops are marked with braided copper-and-fibre cords as places where the Current has manifested with particular force.

Offences against Electra are not simple disobedience: they are acts that soil or interrupt the Current. Deliberately polluting water or earth, falsifying a discovery, wasting energy, altering a body without consent, using electricity to torture or refusing aid when a life can be saved are profanations. The gravest is deliberately cutting a person’s vital current without an extreme cause. Even when a death prevents a greater evil, it is not considered a pure act: it requires confession, restitution and purification.

We believe that those who accumulate such offences lose harmony with Electra. They suffer unease, trembling, nightmares, inexplicable errors and breakdowns around them. We do not claim that every accident is divine punishment, but a life built against the Current eventually creates noise and destruction. Repeat offenders may be barred from rituals and, in the gravest cases, expelled from the order.

The faithful do not expect immunity from lightning or constant miracles. We ask for clarity of mind, steadiness in the hands, endurance in the face of pain and the insight to understand bodies and machines. When a miracle comes, we say Electra has not rewarded us: she has simply found a clean path through us.
