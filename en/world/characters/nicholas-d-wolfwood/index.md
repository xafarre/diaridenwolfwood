Nicholas D. Wolfwood is a lean priest of about forty, with Japanese features,
dark hair and weary eyes that his austere clothes cannot quite conceal. He
dresses in black, without a trace of luxury to invite a second glance, and
moves with the discretion of someone who would rather not look like a threat.

He introduces himself as a priest and healer. He proved it in prison when the
guards ordered him to treat Khor’s wound: his modified left hand cauterised it
amid electrical arcs and the smell of burnt flesh. The demonstration answered
the most immediate question; the others remain open.
