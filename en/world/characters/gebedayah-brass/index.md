Gebedayah Brass stands two metres sixty tall and makes any room feel smaller.
His enormous shoulders, prominent muscles and coarse skin, worn like old
leather, suggest a body built to endure what would force any other man to find
a machine. His years in factories have also damaged his hearing.

He is a second-generation OG-207 and works in a metalworks among furnaces,
loads, hammers and industrial vehicles. He wastes no words and endures each new
setback with the patience of someone who has always lived under a factory
whistle. In prison he preferred Contart, but he kept his word and accepted
Suelex when the group made its decision.
