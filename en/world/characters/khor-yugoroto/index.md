Khor Yugoroto is a young black-panther mutant, about twenty years old and close
to six foot three. He walks upright and has yellow eyes, a scar across one eye
and retractable claws. Black fur covers his entire body, but he has no tail.

He arrived at the prison dressed as a nobleman: from a distance, the silhouette
and bearing were convincing; up close, the materials and finish betrayed the
imitation. He endured the cauterisation of a wound without losing his composure
and defended a small figurine when the officers gathered up their possessions
for incineration. He was then the first to choose Suelex. He has yet to explain
what he is looking for there, but he does not seem to act without a purpose.
