“We have to go up after her,” I say.

Toni refuses. He points towards the exit and reminds us why we were sent into the building.

“This is a reconnaissance mission. We’re to find out what’s here and report back. We’ve seen enough to do that. We’re leaving.”

Mònica nods at once, over and over. She glances towards the stairs, still frightened by the fighting, and agrees with him again.

“Yes. We have to leave. Those are our orders.”

Her insistence strikes me as fear rather than discipline. The other group wants to get out too. I look at the blood trail leading upstairs.

“There’s a woman up there. She might still be alive.”

“And we’re supposed to report back,” Toni insists. “That was the mission.”

I turn to Khor and Gebedayah.

“I’m going on. If someone is dying up there, I won’t let them die because we did nothing. Will you come with me?”

Both agree. With them beside me, I address Toni again.

“Go and report, then. Tell them what we’ve found and ask for reinforcements. We’re going after her.”

We part at the foot of the stairs. Khor and Gebedayah climb with me, following the blood to a walkway flanked by metal grilles that look like ventilation openings. Some are open. Khor tries to close one, but Gebedayah stops us with a warning.

“Over there. Something’s moving.”

The beasts spring at us. We abandon the search to defend ourselves, watching the mouths coming towards us. This time we drive them back without much difficulty and press on.

The next hall opens before us, spacious and half wrecked. Light falls across what looks like a marble floor; no columns obscure the Gothic arches, the large door on one side or the empty frame on the other. I search among the debris for the trail, but when I raise my eyes I see beasts clinging high on the wall.

They are waiting for us.

<figure class="diary-illustration">
  <img src="{{ '/assets/images/diary/acte-6/besties-paret/besties-paret.png' | relative_url }}" alt="Nicholas spots the beasts waiting high on the wall." loading="lazy">
</figure>

The attack seems coordinated. We defend ourselves again and manage to drive them off, but the silence they leave behind is chilling. While they are in front of us, at least we know where they are. Now I have to look up again before I can resume searching for the missing woman’s trail.

We approach the opening where the light comes in. Gebedayah stops to listen.

“Can’t you hear that? A very high note. Like a violin.”

Khor hears nothing. Neither do I. Near the doorway, a beast watches us from against the wall without moving. Gebedayah throws a stone at it and misses; the animal does not flinch. It neither retreats nor lunges at us. We continue cautiously, keeping it in sight.

Khor covers our rear. Another horde appears through a doorway, but this time the creatures do not run. They stand still, just like the one beside us.

<figure class="diary-illustration">
  <img src="{{ '/assets/images/diary/acte-6/porta-besties-immobils/porta-besties-immobils.png' | relative_url }}" alt="The group approaches the doorway while the beasts watch without moving." loading="lazy">
</figure>

As we cross the threshold, I recognise the dome we saw from the bridge. Beneath weathered steel frames and broken glass, we tread across hardened mud. Then I hear the note: high, just as Gebedayah described it.

“Now I hear it.”

But they no longer can. Not even Gebedayah. I do not know what has changed since we came through the door.

We move between roughly five clay vessels, open like hemispherical plant pots, with columns of the same material rising from them towards the glass. I try to make sense of what I am seeing while Khor watches behind us. He is the only one who turns in time.

<figure class="diary-illustration">
  <img src="{{ '/assets/images/diary/acte-6/bestia-violi/bestia-violi.png' | relative_url }}" alt="Khor spots the creature perched between two broken structural columns near the dome entrance." loading="lazy">
</figure>

A mass some three metres across launches itself from two broken columns near the entrance and drops on us. As it moves, its soft body bends like a bean between four legs that look immensely hard. Its head is set in the centre, its wings too small to support it; I see them vibrating and recognise the violin note.

Khor’s claws tear a huge wound in that soft body. The beast turns and runs towards the far end of the room. I draw out my left hand, activate the weapon and extend my arm, tracking it until its head lines up with my finger. I fire.

The shot shatters its head.

<figure class="diary-illustration">
  <img src="{{ '/assets/images/diary/acte-6/tret-letal/tret-letal.png' | relative_url }}" alt="Nicholas fires from his mechanical left hand, shattering the creature’s head." loading="lazy">
</figure>

Khor and Gebedayah look at me in surprise. There is no time to say anything. A second creature, larger still, catches us unprepared. It strikes me first.

I never see where the next will come from.

## The rescue

Nicholas falls unconscious. Khor asks Gebedayah to hold the beast back while he prepares to carry his companion. He looks for a way to get him out, but before he can escape a blow sends him down beside Nicholas.

Gebedayah is left alone facing the animal, his clothes dirty and his face drenched in sweat. His breathing is laboured. Nicholas and Khor do not get up, and he is already exhausted when he hears the shout from the doorway:

“Don’t move!”

He obeys. An enormous ball of fire crosses the room and strikes the beast.

<figure class="diary-illustration">
  <img src="{{ '/assets/images/diary/acte-6/bola-de-foc/bola-de-foc.png' | relative_url }}" alt="Dirty and exhausted, Gebedayah stands still as a fireball flies towards the beast, with reinforcements in the background." loading="lazy">
</figure>

Smoke still escapes from the gloves of a young woman who stops in the entrance. It rises along her forearms, where narrow slots open through the leather, and disperses before her pale face, bare of hair and eyebrows. Two red stripes cross each eye. Beside her, an older woman with similar features watches the beast; both wear light veils and red clothing that leaves much of their torsos exposed.

The machine gun thunders before the animal can recover. The gunner operates it from a metal body supported on four articulated legs; tubes and cables join his human torso to the prosthesis. Ammunition feeds from the reservoir behind him, and the burst finishes the creature off.

<figure class="diary-illustration">
  <img src="{{ '/assets/images/diary/acte-6/rafega-metralladora/rafega-metralladora.png' | relative_url }}" alt="A man integrated into a four-legged spider prosthesis fires a machine gun inside the dome." loading="lazy">
</figure>

Toni enters with Samón, who still carries a battle axe in each hand. Heavy armour covers his body, and his helmet reaches down to his mouth. Behind them, a man in grey advances among black-suited escorts with dark glasses and rifles.

The beast no longer moves. Gebedayah remains standing between his two fallen companions and the people who have just entered. Nicholas and Khor are still unconscious.
